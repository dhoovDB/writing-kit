**Task given to Claude:** "Explain what Net Interest Margin is to a product
manager joining a community bank for the first time. They understand software
but have never worked in financial services."

---

**Concept:** Net Interest Margin (NIM)

**What it is:**
- The spread between what the bank earns on loans and investments and what
  it pays depositors
- Expressed as a percentage of earning assets
- The primary profitability metric for most community banks

**Why it matters to a PM:**
- Almost every product decision at a community bank touches NIM somewhere
- A product that attracts low-cost deposits expands it
- A clunky loan application that loses borrowers compresses it
- NIM at most community banks sits between 3% and 4%

**Audience:** A PM joining from a software or tech background, no finance
experience assumed
